# Ayman Kamel — Scientific Profile

Scientific Computing | Computational Mechanics | Research Software | Scientific AI Evaluation

## Biography

My background is in mechanical engineering and scientific computing, with interests in numerical modeling, computational physics and verification of scientific software. My current project work in Kepler focuses on developing and reviewing scientific benchmark prototypes and evaluating AI-generated scientific solutions against mathematical and computational reference checks.

The work includes Python-based coupled heat-transfer and cluster-kinetics models, numerical integration, optimization, generalized eigenvalue problems and automated verification. I emphasize conservation laws, numerical convergence, explicit assumptions and reproducible command-line execution.

My implemented applications currently focus on materials physics and computational mechanics. Their methods provide a foundation for work in computational geophysics and environmental modeling, particularly heat transport and dynamical systems.

## Selected Kepler work

- Thermal-memory prototype: conducting slab coupled to Al–Zr integer-cluster kinetics, positive backward-Euler and independent BDF solvers, command-line evaluation and bounded optimization.
- Numerical verification: conservation, detailed balance, selected resolution refinements, structured input contracts, diagnostics and execution journals.
- Scientific AI evaluation: generalized eigenvalue analysis, repeated eigenspaces, projected perturbations, directional derivatives, finite-difference and basis-rotation checks.

## Tools and methods

Python, NumPy, SciPy, pytest, ODE integration, heat transfer, optimization, numerical linear algebra, command-line workflows and automated scientific checks.

## Scope

Current Kepler applications use synthetic materials inputs and mechanical models. Experimental calibration, complete all-scenario convergence, global optimality and platform acceptance are not established. Direct processing of observational Earth-science datasets is not claimed. Recent thermal-memory execution is documented on Windows/PowerShell; Linux execution and container validation remain unverified for that prototype.

أعمل في الحوسبة العلمية والميكانيكا الحاسوبية، مع اهتمام بالنمذجة العددية والتحقق من صحة البرامج العلمية. يركز عملي الحالي في مشروع Kepler على تطوير ومراجعة نماذج أولية لمهام علمية، وتقييم حلول الذكاء الاصطناعي بمقارنتها بحسابات مرجعية واختبارات رياضية وعددية.

التطبيقات المنفذة حاليًا في فيزياء المواد والميكانيكا، مع صلة منهجية بالجيوفيزياء والنمذجة البيئية عبر انتقال الحرارة والأنظمة الديناميكية والتحقق العددي. لا أدعي خبرة مباشرة غير موثقة ببيانات الرصد في علوم الأرض أو نشرًا علميًا محكمًا لهذه النماذج.
